403 Forbidden, ketika mencoba download driver printer Epson L1210 series menggunakan wget

TL;DR

Gw mau coba install driver printer Epson L1210 series di ubuntu, tapi pas gw download drivernya ga ke direct download otomatis melainkan malah nge-read packagenya di browser

Download

Downloadnya disini, pilih yang linux

Pilih yang Epson Printer Utility for Linux

Abis itu klik download, nanti muncul kek gini

Solusi

Pake wget, tapi 403 Forbidden?
